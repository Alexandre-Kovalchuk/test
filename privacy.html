1. Introduction

SDOMEN (hereinafter referred to as "we," "our," or "the Company") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Google Ads requirements.

This Privacy Policy applies to the website https://SDOMEN.com/ and explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

2. What Data Do We Collect?

When you use our website, we may process the following categories of data:

Personal Data

Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number (if you submit contact forms or subscribe to newsletters).

Technical Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device information.

Cookie Data and Similar Technologies (see Section 7 for details).

Analytics Data: Pages visited, time spent on the site, referral source (via Google Analytics and other tools).

Advertising Data: Interactions with ad campaigns (Google Ads, retargeting).

3. Purposes of Data Processing

We use your data for the following purposes:

Website functionality and user experience improvement.

Traffic analysis (using Google Analytics).

Displaying relevant ads (including Google Ads and remarketing).

Sending email newsletters (only with your consent).

Processing inquiries submitted via contact forms.

4. How Do We Store and Protect Data?

Data is stored on secure servers within the European Union or with GDPR-compliant providers.

We implement technical and organizational security measures, including encryption, access controls, and regular audits.

Advertising data (Google Ads) is anonymized where possible.

5. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a data subject, you have the right to:

Request access to your personal data.

Request erasure ("right to be forgotten") where no legal basis for retention exists.

Restrict processing under certain conditions.

Data portability (receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format).

Withdraw consent (e.g., for email marketing or cookies).

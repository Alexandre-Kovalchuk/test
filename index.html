About Us

We are a close-knit team of appliance repair professionals committed to one thing: doing honest, high-quality work that actually makes a difference in people’s homes.

Founded by technicians — not marketers — our company grew from hands-on experience in repairing what others called "irreparable." We’ve seen the frustration that comes when appliances break down, especially when the first answer clients hear is: "Just replace it." But more often than not, a well-executed repair is the smarter, more sustainable, and more affordable solution.

Our approach is rooted in transparency, technical precision, and human connection. Every service request begins with clear communication — what’s wrong, what can be done, what it will cost — and ends with a solution that not only works today, but lasts. We don’t believe in rushed diagnostics, vague invoices, or unnecessary part swaps. Instead, we invest in proper fault detection, genuine parts when replacements are needed, and skilled craftsmanship. Whether we’re servicing a compact apartment machine or a large-capacity refrigerator, we apply the same level of care.

More than anything, we believe in restoring trust in the repair process. We’re not just fixing machines — we’re helping households function smoothly again. That’s what keeps our clients coming back, and why most of our new customers come through word-of-mouth.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my appliance is worth repairing?

We always perform a detailed diagnosis before recommending any repair. If the cost of fixing your appliance approaches the value of replacement, we’ll tell you honestly — no pressure, no sales tricks.

What types of appliances do you repair?

We specialize in washing machines (front and top load) and refrigerators (freestanding and built-in models). Other appliance types may be accepted on a case-by-case basis.

Do you offer warranty on your repairs?

Yes. All repairs include a service warranty — typically 3 to 6 months depending on the part and complexity. You’ll receive full documentation.

Are your technicians certified?

Yes. All our technicians undergo ongoing training and are certified in appliance diagnostics and electrical safety protocols.

What are your service hours?

We operate Monday through Saturday, from 08:00 to 19:00. Emergency appointments may be arranged upon request.

How long does a standard repair take?

Many issues can be resolved within 1–2 hours on-site. If parts need to be ordered, we’ll provide a clear timeline before proceeding.

Do you charge for diagnostics?

Yes, a flat €40 fee applies for a full diagnostic visit — but if you proceed with the repair, this amount is deducted from the total.

Do I need to prepare anything before the technician arrives?

Please ensure the appliance is accessible and there’s a safe working space around it. No need to disassemble or unplug anything — we’ll take care of that.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept card payments, bank transfers, and cash. Invoices are provided for every service.
